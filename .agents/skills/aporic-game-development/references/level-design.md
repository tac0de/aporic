# Level design

Define the player's starting knowledge, goal, available verbs, constraints, and intended route or set of routes. Sketch a beat sequence: orient, introduce, practice, vary, test, and resolve. Treat it as a hypothesis; genre and player skill may call for a different rhythm.

Make a playable blockout before final art. Test scale, traversal time, sight lines, landmarks, cover or obstacles, encounter spacing, safe recovery points, and unintended bypasses from the player camera. Use light, shape, sound, contrast, and framing to guide attention, while leaving room for meaningful choice. Check whether a player can distinguish an intentional secret from a confusing route.

Observe a cold playthrough without coaching. Note wrong turns, repeated failures, skipped beats, and moments of discovery; ask the player what they expected after the attempt. Revise one consequential issue at a time and replay the complete path. Keep alternate routes, checkpoints, and challenge changes consistent with the core loop.

Reference: [Epic's level blockout and early playtesting guide](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/designer-01-project-setup-and-level-blockout-in-unreal-engine). Its engine commands are examples; apply the blockout principle in the project's chosen engine.
