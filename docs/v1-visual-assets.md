# V1 palace work-map asset

The observatory uses one generated raster background as a replaceable
presentation asset. Agent identity, position, state, and labels are rendered
from the live literal snapshot; the artwork carries no authority and does not
alter kernel replay.

Generation mode: built-in OpenAI image generation.

Saved as `crates/aporic-observatory/assets/images/palace-courtyard.png`.

Prompt:

> Use case: UI background asset for a live software-agent workflow visualization. Create a clean, elegant, simplified isometric illustration of a Joseon dynasty palace complex as an information map, 16:9 landscape. Four visually distinct but historically inspired work zones connected by broad stone paths: a small left gate and waiting pavilion, a central open execution courtyard, a right-side verification office, and a grand completion hall at the top center. Premium editorial vector-like digital illustration with restrained shapes, crisp architectural silhouettes, subtle paper texture, and clear spatial hierarchy. Slightly elevated three-quarter view. Palette: muted indigo roofs, warm ivory stone, restrained dancheong teal and vermilion, soft dusk background. Keep the courtyard uncluttered and paths highly legible for overlaid animated character markers. No people, no labels, no letters, no signage, no UI, no realistic cinematic lighting, no fog, no photorealism, no fantasy, no Chinese imperial or Japanese architecture, no watermark. The result should feel like a beautiful strategy-map illustration rather than a movie still or game HUD.
